# Membership FAQ

**Q: What are the membership tiers?**
A: Community (evenings and weekends), Full (24/7 badge access), and Studio (Full plus a dedicated half-bench). Day passes exist for visitors working alongside a member.

**Q: How do checkouts work?**
A: Green-tag machines are open after orientation. Yellow-tag machines need a specific checkout session, bookable at the front desk. Red-tag machines are facilitator-operated only.

**Q: Can I bring a guest?**
A: Yes, up to two guests who stay within arm's reach and off all machines. Guests doing hands-on work need a day pass.

**Q: Is there storage?**
A: Each member gets one labeled tote on the storage wall. Projects left on benches overnight without a parking permit slip migrate to the free-material shelf on Fridays.

**Q: How do I freeze or cancel?**
A: Email the front desk before the 25th of the month. Freezes are free for up to three months per year.

**Q: What happens if I break a machine?**
A: Report it immediately, no charge for honest wear and honest accidents. Unreported damage discovered later is treated much less warmly.
