# 3D Printer Guide

## Getting started
All members may use the Prusa MK4 printers after a 20-minute checkout. The Bambu X1C requires the advanced checkout because of its enclosure and higher temperatures.

## Filament rules
House PLA and PETG are free for prints under 100 grams. Larger prints use your own spool or filament purchased at the front desk. Nylon and carbon-filled filaments are only allowed on the X1C with a hardened nozzle fitted.

## Bed adhesion
Wash the PEI sheet with dish soap and water weekly, and wipe with isopropyl alcohol between prints. Glue stick is required for PETG to prevent it bonding permanently to the sheet.

## Failed prints
If a print detaches or spaghettis, stop the job, clear the bed, and log the failure in the printer logbook. Repeated failures of the same model usually mean the first layer is too high or the model needs supports.

## Overnight printing
Overnight prints are allowed on the Prusa farm only. Enclosure printers must not run unattended. Label your job with your member ID and estimated finish time.
