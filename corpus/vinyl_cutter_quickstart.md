# Vinyl Cutter Quickstart

Load vinyl glossy side up, pinch rollers on the white marks. Blade depth: barely scratch a test square, kiss-cut not through-cut. Send from the studio laptop, weed on the green mat with the hook tools, transfer tape is on the shelf. Full guide is in the binder next to the machine.
