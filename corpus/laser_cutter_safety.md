# Laser Cutter Safety and Materials

## Absolute rules
Never leave the laser running unattended, even for a minute. Know where the CO2 extinguisher is before you press start. The extraction fan must be running before the job starts and for two minutes after it ends.

## Approved materials
Plywood up to 6mm, MDF up to 6mm, acrylic (cast preferred) up to 8mm, cardboard, paper, leather (vegetable tanned only), and anodized aluminum for marking.

## Forbidden materials
PVC and anything containing chlorine (produces chlorine gas that destroys lungs and the machine), polycarbonate (catches fire), ABS (melts and smells terrible), fiberglass, and any mirrored acrylic face-down. If you are not sure what a material is, do not cut it. Ask a facilitator.

## Focus and power
Focus with the acrylic gauge before every job. Use the materials chart taped to the lid for power and speed settings. Start with lower power and a test cut in a corner rather than guessing high.

## Fire response
Small flare-ups that die when the head moves on are normal. A sustained flame means: open lid, pause job, smother with the fire blanket. Do not use water on the optics.
