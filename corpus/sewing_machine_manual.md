# Sewing and Textiles Station Manual

## Machines
Two Juki straight-stitch machines, one Brother overlocker, and one heavy-duty walking-foot machine for canvas and leather. The walking-foot machine requires a five-minute checkout because it will happily sew through your finger.

## Needles and thread
Change needles when you switch fabric weight. Universal 80/12 for cottons, 90/14 for denim, leather point for leather. Bent or dull needles go in the red sharps tin, never the regular bin.

## Overlocker threading
Do not rethread the overlocker from scratch. Tie new threads onto the old ones and pull them through, then rethread only the needles. If you break the threading completely, see the laminated diagram or ask, budget twenty minutes either way.

## Fabric scraps
Scraps larger than an A4 sheet go back in the community fabric bins sorted by type. Smaller scraps go in the stuffing bag for community projects.

## Irons
Irons switch off automatically after ten minutes, but turn them off yourself anyway. Empty the water tank after steaming or it scales up.
