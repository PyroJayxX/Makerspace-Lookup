cnc router - marco's notes (sorry will clean these up later)

feeds n speeds: 6mm 2flute upcut in ply -> 18000rpm, 2000mm/min, doc 3mm. birch ply chips out on top face w/ upcut, use downcut or compression for finish passes

DONT run the 1/8 bit deeper than 4mm/pass it WILL snap, we lost 3 last month

workholding: tape+CA method or the tnut clamps. screws into spoilboard = fine but MARK THEM so nobody surfaces into a screw again (asking for tuesday incident guy)

always zero Z off the spoilboard not the stock if ur cutting through, stock thickness lies

dust boot stays ON. yes it blocks the view. use the camera

vacuum table pump needs 5 min warmup in cold weather or it wont pull

if the spindle makes a screaming noise at startup its the collet, stop and re-seat it. dont just turn it up louder lol

emergency stop is the big red one, resets from the side panel. if u estop mid job re-home before restarting or itll air cut offset
