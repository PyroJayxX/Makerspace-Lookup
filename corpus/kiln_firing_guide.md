# Kiln Firing Guide

Bisque firings run Tuesdays and Fridays, glaze firings run Saturdays, loaded by the ceramics facilitator only. Members never open a kiln themselves; the interlock is not a suggestion.

Work must be bone dry before bisque. If the piece feels cool against your cheek, it is still wet and it will explode and take neighbors with it. When in doubt, give it another week on the greenware shelf.

Maximum wall thickness is 25mm. Hollow forms need a vent hole. Sculptural work thicker than the limit needs facilitator sign-off and a slow-fire slot.

Glazes: house glazes are food-safe when applied per the chart. Keep glaze 5mm clear of the foot or the piece will fuse to the shelf, and shelf damage is chargeable. Wipe wax resist spills immediately.

Put your member ID on the greenware shelf tag, not carved into the piece bottom unless you enjoy sanding.

Firing fees are by shelf volume, posted on the ceramics board. Community projects fire free on the last Saturday of the month.
