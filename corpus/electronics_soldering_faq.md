# Electronics Bench and Soldering FAQ

**Q: Do I need a checkout to use the soldering irons?**
A: No, the basic irons are open to all members. The hot air rework station and the reflow oven require the electronics checkout.

**Q: What temperature should I set the iron?**
A: 320C for leaded solder, 350C for lead-free. If you need more than 380C, your tip is probably oxidized. Clean it on the brass sponge and re-tin it.

**Q: Is lead-free solder mandatory?**
A: House solder is lead-free. You may bring leaded solder, but you must use the fume extractor and wash your hands afterward. No exceptions on the extractor.

**Q: Can I etch PCBs here?**
A: No. We do not have chemical disposal for etchant. Use the PCB mill or order boards externally.

**Q: Someone left components out. Can I use them?**
A: Parts in the labeled member bins belong to those members. Anything in the grey "free bin" is fair game. When in doubt, ask on the notice board.

**Q: My circuit works on the bench supply but not on battery. Why?**
A: Usually voltage sag under load or a missing common ground. Check both before asking for help, then ask for help. Facilitators like this question.
