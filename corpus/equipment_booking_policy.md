# Equipment Booking Policy

## What needs booking
The laser cutter, CNC router, vinyl cutter, walking-foot sewing machine, and the recording booth all run on the booking calendar. Everything else is walk-up.

## Booking rules
Book in one-hour blocks, maximum three consecutive hours, maximum eight hours per week per member on any single machine. The week resets Monday morning.

## No-shows
If you are more than fifteen minutes late, your slot opens to anyone waiting. Three no-shows in a month pauses your booking privileges for two weeks. Cancel ahead of time and it never counts against you.

## Peak hours
Weekday evenings 6pm to 9pm are peak. During peak, the three-hour maximum drops to two hours on the laser cutter only, because it is the bottleneck machine.

## Maintenance windows
The first Monday morning of each month is blocked for maintenance on all bookable machines. Recurring bookings never override maintenance windows.
