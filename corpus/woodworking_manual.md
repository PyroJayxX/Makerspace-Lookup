# Woodworking Shop Manual

## Table saw
The table saw may only be used after completing the green-tag orientation. Always use the riving knife and blade guard for through cuts. Push sticks are mandatory for any rip narrower than 150mm. The blade must come to a complete stop before you reach over it, no exceptions.

## Bandsaw
Set the upper guide 5 to 10mm above the workpiece. Relieve tight curves with relief cuts before following the line. If the blade drifts, ask a facilitator to check tension and tracking rather than forcing the cut.

## Planer and jointer
Boards must be at least 300mm long before passing through the planer. Never plane reclaimed wood without checking for nails and screws with the metal detector hanging by the door. Jointer cuts deeper than 1.5mm per pass are not allowed.

## Finishing area
Oily rags must go into the red metal bin with the self-closing lid. They can self-ignite in open bins. Solvent finishes may only be applied in the spray corner with extraction running.

## Cleanup
Sweep your station, empty the dust extractor bag if it is more than two-thirds full, and return shared tools to the shadow board before leaving.
