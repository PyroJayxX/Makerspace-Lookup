# Resin Printing Safety

## Why resin is different
Uncured resin is a skin sensitizer. You might handle it fine ten times and develop a permanent allergy on the eleventh. Treat every drop as hazardous until it is fully cured.

## Mandatory PPE
Nitrile gloves (not latex, resin goes through latex), safety glasses, and the resin room extraction running. Long sleeves recommended when pouring.

## Workflow
Pour resin in the resin room only. Print, then wash parts in the two-stage IPA bath: dirty tank first, clean tank second. Cure washed parts in the UV station for the time listed on the resin chart. Only fully cured parts may leave the resin room.

## Waste
Used IPA goes in the black jug, never down the drain. Cure any resin-contaminated paper towels in the UV station before binning them. Empty resin bottles get one clear-water rinse, cure the rinse water in the sun tray, then recycle the bottle.

## Spills
Small spills: wipe with paper towel, cure the towel, then bin. Large spills: cordon the area and call a facilitator. Do not mop; mopping spreads it.
