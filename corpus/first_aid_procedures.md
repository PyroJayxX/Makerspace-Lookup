# First Aid Procedures

## Kits and equipment
First aid kits are mounted at the front desk, the wood shop entrance, and the electronics bench. The AED and eye-wash station are by the front desk. The burn kit lives in the resin room.

## Minor injuries
Clean, dress, and log every injury in the incident book, even small splinters and nicks. The log is how we spot dangerous patterns, not how we assign blame.

## Serious injuries
Call 911 first, then the facilitator phone. Do not move anyone with a suspected back or head injury unless they are in immediate danger. Send someone to the street entrance to guide the ambulance in.

## Eye injuries
Do not rub. Flush at the eye-wash station for a full fifteen minutes by the clock, then get medical attention. For chemical splashes, bring the container or its photo to the hospital.

## Cuts from machines
Apply firm direct pressure with the green trauma dressings. If bleeding soaks through, add layers on top, never remove the first dressing. Machine stays red-tagged until the safety committee clears it, no matter how minor the cut.

## After any incident
The facilitator files an incident report within 24 hours. The involved machine gets checked before returning to service.
